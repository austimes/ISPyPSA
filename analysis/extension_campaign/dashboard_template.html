Grid Pathways Monitor

Emissions-capped capacity expansion for the National Electricity Market, 2030 to 2060. Each chain carries one demand trajectory under one emissions-cap schedule, solved milestone by milestone with the fleet carried forward.

Milestone gates

A milestone joins the pathway menu only if it is solver-certified, tracks its cap within one per cent, and sheds no load. A cell that sheds load is a measurement of where the cap stops being reachable, not a pathway anyone can follow.

Where the caps bite

Realised emissions intensity against the shadow price of the cap that produced it. The price is what one further tonne of abatement costs the system, and it climbs far faster than the intensity falls.

Cost against demand, by pressure setting

Annual resource cost (new-build annuities, fixed and variable operating cost and fuel; carbon payments and the load-shedding penalty removed) against delivered demand, one line per pressure setting. Hollow markers are boundary cells.

Average cost against realised intensity, by trajectory

The same cost per delivered MWh, placed on the intensity each cell actually reached, so price chains and cap chains sit on one curve per demand trajectory.

What the fleet generates

Annual sent-out energy by carrier at each milestone. Load shedding appears as its own band, so a pathway that cannot serve its demand shows the gap rather than hiding it.
